## 2. What data we collect

**When you create an account**
- Email address
- Password (stored only as a cryptographic hash — we never see or store your actual password)
- Display name
- Your role (client or practitioner)
- Language and display preferences

**If you are a practitioner, additionally**
- Profile information you choose to provide: biography, headline, location, profile photo, cover image, username
- The practices and topics you offer
- Your timezone and working hours
- Services you list: name, description, duration, price, how sessions are delivered
- For in-person or telephone services: the address or phone number you provide (shown only to clients who have a confirmed booking with you)
- Your Stripe account identifier, so payments can be routed to you

**When you book a session**
- Which service, with which practitioner, at what time
- A record of the service as agreed at the time of booking (name, price, duration, delivery method) — kept so that later changes by the practitioner don't alter what was agreed with you
- Booking status and any cancellation, including who cancelled and any note they added

**When you pay**
- The amount, currency, and references identifying the transaction with our payment provider
- **We never receive or store your card details.** These are handled entirely by Stripe.

**When you leave a review**
- Your rating and any text you write
- Reviews are shown publicly, and to the practitioner, **without your name** — identified only as a verified user

**When you contact us**
- Your name, email address, the category of your enquiry and your message. This is sent to us by email and is not stored in our database.

**Automatically**
- Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, including your IP address for rate limiting and abuse prevention
- Essential cookies (see section 8)

**Planned but not yet in use:** video sessions held on the platform. When introduced, we will record who joined a session and when, in order to establish whether a session took place — this is used for refunds where one party did not attend. We will update this policy before that feature launches.

## 7. Your rights

You can:
- **Access your data** — download everything we hold about you, from your account settings
- **Correct it** — edit your profile at any time
- **Delete your account** — from your settings. See below for what this means in practice.
- **Withdraw consent** — turn off marketing emails at any time, in settings
- **Object or restrict** — contact us
- **Complain** — to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни), or the authority in your country of residence

**What deleting your account actually does.** Your login is disabled and your personal details are removed from your profile. Past bookings remain, but with your name replaced — this is so the practitioner or client you dealt with keeps a complete record of their own history. Reviews remain, as they never carried your name. Transaction records are kept where tax law requires it, no longer linked to your identity.

You cannot delete your account while you have upcoming bookings. Cancel or complete them first.
